## Math that was lying

- Unknown roof/HVAC/water heater years no longer inherit year-built as the install date. Age-of-home maintenance covers the unknown.
- Work-optional 4% uses living + **recurring** ownership, not one-shot replacements as an annual lifestyle.
- Keep-house stands now when free cash covers reserved, instead of dating the next overdue system as “the hire.”
- Renters no longer get a phantom roof in reserved. Emergency Fund is no longer the same formula as Stay Afloat.
- An owned house is not treated as paid off unless they say so. Debt Free can take more than one loan on the first outline.
- Missing loan APR is not 0%. The walker uses a labeled typical until they enter the rate.
